The Cedar Ridge Difference

Built to Last, Not Rushed to Finish

We know there are dozens of generic crew trucks around. We distinguish ourselves by holding a higher standard for materials, site cleanliness, and customer service.

Warp-Resistant Sourcing

We select our wood planks individually. No split boards or heavy knotting that will warp or shrink after the first rain.

Proper Post Footings

Every fence post is buried to depth and secured in mixed concrete, ensuring a solid base that withstands high winds and wet ground.

Clean Job Sites

We pick up wood chips, vacuum nails, and sweep your driveway every afternoon. We respect your grass and home perimeter.
