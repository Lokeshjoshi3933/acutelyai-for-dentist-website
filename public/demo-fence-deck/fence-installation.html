Fencing Built for Tennessee Backyards

A high-quality fence does more than mark your boundary lines—it keeps children and pets safe, isolates noise from neighborhood streets, and creates a private sanctuary for your family. At Cedar Ridge Fence & Deck Co., we design and build fences from the ground up, tailored to the specific slopes and clay soil of Middle Tennessee.

Types of Fences We Install

Depending on your budget, neighborhood association rules (HOAs), and design preferences, we offer three main types of fencing materials:

1. Natural Red Cedar & Wood Fencing

Wood is the classic choice for suburban homes. We construct wood fences board-by-board, rather than using flimsy pre-built panels. This allows us to adjust for slopes smoothly, ensuring a neat, consistent top-line. We highly recommend Western Red Cedar because it contains natural oils that resist insect rot, checking, and warping under the Southern humidity.

2. Low-Maintenance Vinyl Fencing

If you prefer a fence that never needs painting, staining, or power-washing, premium vinyl is the ideal option. Our vinyl fences are manufactured with thick profiles and titanium dioxide UV inhibitors to prevent chalking, yellowing, or cracking under hot Tennessee sunbeams.

3. Black Aluminum & Chain-Link Safety Fences

Perfect for swimming pool enclosures or large acreage borders. Black aluminum fencing provides a classic wrought-iron aesthetic with zero rust risks, maintaining open sightlines while satisfying local safety building codes.

Our Fence Installation Process

A typical residential fence replacement takes 2 to 4 days depending on length:

Day 1: Demolition & Post Digging

We remove your old leaning fence, haul it away, mark boundaries, and dig post holes to code-compliant depths, anchoring structural supports in fresh concrete.

Day 2: Framing & Railing

We install the heavy horizontal stringers (framing rails) using heavy structural fasteners. This builds the robust skeleton of your fence.

Day 3: Boarding & Custom Gates

We nail individual cedar or wood pickets, align double drive gates with heavy-duty hinges, and perform site sweepings to clean up chips, nails, and debris.
