Outdoor Living Built for Franklin Families

A deck is more than an addition—it's an extension of your family living space. Whether you're hosting neighborhood cookouts, relaxing in the evenings, or watching the kids play in the yard, your deck must be solid, secure, and built to withstand the elements. At Cedar Ridge Fence & Deck Co., we combine heavy-duty framing, structural permits management, and premium decking materials to build decks that last decades.

Decking Material Options

We work with the two leading decking categories, letting you balance budget and maintenance requirements:

1. Premium Composite Decking (Trex & TimberTech)

Composite decking is the gold standard for modern homes. Made from a blend of recycled wood fibers and durable plastics, composite boards never warp, splinter, rot, or split. There is no need for annual power washing, staining, or sanding. Our composite decks feature hidden fastener systems so there are no exposed screw heads on the floor surface.

2. Pressure-Treated Natural Southern Pine

For homeowners who love the authentic look and feel of natural wood, pressure-treated pine is a classic, cost-effective option. We use high-grade structural timber that has been infused with preservative agents to prevent termite damage. Natural wood decks require sealing or staining every two to three years to maintain their beauty and prevent dry-rot.

Deck Structural Standards & Safety

A deck's durability comes from what is underneath. Many builders cut costs on the frame. We hold ourselves to strict residential carpentry building codes:

6x6 post supports (never wimpier 4x4s)
Solid concrete footings poured below frost line
Double-ply rim joist beams for load strength
Heavy-duty steel joist hangers & hurricane ties
Proper flashing membranes at house ledger connections
Administrative management of HOA & building permits

Deck Stairs & Custom Railings

A deck's staircase and railing outline its design character. We install beautiful, secure railings that satisfy code load requirements (withstand 200 lbs of lateral pressure):

  • Classic Wood Railings: Styled to match the deck timber, providing a clean traditional border.
  • Modern Aluminum Balusters: Sleek black metal spindles set in wood frames, offering a striking contrast and better sightlines to your yard.
  • Full-Composite Railings: Matching post wraps, rails, and pickets that are rot-free and look uniform with Trex/TimberTech floors.

Our Custom Deck Building Process

Building a deck is a major project. We maintain communication during each phase:

Phase 1: Design, Permit & Layout (1-2 Weeks Before)

We compile dimensional drawings, submit documents to local Franklin/Williamson County permit offices, order materials, and schedule the utilities marking visit.

Phase 2: Footings & Structural Framing (Days 1-2)

Our crew digs holes, pours concrete footings, installs heavy 6x6 posts, mounts the ledger board safely to the house rim, and frames the joist skeleton.

Phase 3: Floor Boarding, Stairs & Trim (Days 3-5)

We install the composite or pine floorboards, construct structural staircases with solid landing pads, set railings, clean the yard, and schedule the final city inspection.
