Case Studies of Recent Construction

Every backyard is unique. Read the stories behind some of our favorite fence and deck builds in Middle Tennessee, showcasing our attention to detail from demolition to finishing.

After: Western Red Cedar
Franklin, TN

Backyard Privacy Fence

Service: Privacy Wood Fencing
Timeline: 3 Business Days
Materials: 6' Cedar Boards
Scope: 180 Linear Feet

The Story & Challenge

The homeowner's previous fence had rotted spruce panels that were leaning severely after storm winds, making the backyard unsafe for their two active dogs. The gate was also dragging and difficult to close.

Our Solution

We tore down the rotted fence, dug fresh post holes to a depth of 24 inches, and anchored them in concrete. We then hand-built a Western Red Cedar board-on-board privacy fence. We fabricated a sag-proof double gate with structural metal framing and self-latching locks, ensuring peace of mind for the dogs.

Brentwood, TN

Cedar Fence Replacement

Service: Wood Fence Installation
Timeline: 2 Business Days
Materials: Western Red Cedar
Scope: 220 Linear Feet

The Story & Challenge

An old split-rail fence was decaying and losing its shape, failing to keep local deer from entering the homeowner's flower beds. They wanted a classic boundary that kept small pets inside without blocking the view of their beautiful wooded lawn.

Our Solution

We designed a custom 3-rail Western Red Cedar fence, reinforced with black wire mesh backing. This mesh is almost invisible from a distance, maintaining sightlines, but forms a secure barrier that prevents small dogs from squeezing through and deters woodland critters.

After: Trex Composite Deck
Spring Hill, TN

Composite Deck Build

Service: Composite Deck Building
Timeline: 5 Business Days
Materials: Trex Rocky Harbor
Scope: 16' x 20' Deck Area

The Story & Challenge

The original builder-grade deck stairs were cracking and the landing area was tiny, barely fitting a grill. The family wanted a spacious, low-maintenance outdoor living room to dining table layout.

Our Solution

We demolished the old framing, set heavy 6x6 post footings in deep concrete pads, and framed a beautiful 320 sq ft deck. We used Trex composite boards in Rocky Harbor with hidden fasteners. We installed sleek black aluminum railings and low-voltage stair risers for safety at night.

Thompson’s Station, TN

Pool Safety Fence

Service: Pool Safety Enclosure
Timeline: 2 Business Days
Materials: Black Aluminum Pickets
Scope: 240 Linear Feet

The Story & Challenge

Homeowners were finishing a beautiful new swimming pool and needed an HOA-approved, code-compliant security enclosure before the county inspector would sign off on pool permits.

Our Solution

We designed and installed a premium black aluminum fence that met all local child safety height and picket gap standards. We installed self-closing, magnetic-latching gates and anchored the posts to withstand wet lawn edges, ensuring compliance and peace of mind.

Remodeled Railing
Nolensville, TN

Deck Railing Upgrade

Service: Deck Railing Remodel
Timeline: 2 Business Days
Materials: Matte Black Aluminum
Scope: 86 Linear Feet

The Story & Challenge

The original deck frame and pine floorboards were in sturdy shape, but the wood railings had split, rotted, and wobbled at the corner posts, posing a safety hazard for their kids.

Our Solution

We removed the old rotted wood rails. We reinforced the corner post joints to the deck frame using heavy steel structural lag bolts. We then installed a modern, commercial-grade black aluminum railing system that never needs painting and ensures structural security.
