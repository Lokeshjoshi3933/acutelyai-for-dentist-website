Meet Mark Ellison

Mark started Cedar Ridge Fence & Deck Co. after working over a decade in residential carpentry and outdoor construction. Our Integrity

The Cedar Ridge Standards

When you hire us to construct a deck or install a privacy fence, we hold ourselves accountable to four core building practices:

Clear Fixed Quotes

Our written estimates contain full materials details, timeline benchmarks, and labor fees. We guarantee zero hidden costs or surprise surcharges once we start digging.

Hand-Picked Materials

We source timber locally, checking boards individually to filter out knots, splits, or warping. We build with premium Western Red Cedar and Trex composite boards.

Safe Post Footings

We set fence posts to a code-depth of 24 inches (and decks even deeper) using structural concrete footings. Our fences stand straight against Middle Tennessee storm winds.

Clean Job Sites

We pick up wood chips, vacuum metal fasteners, and sweep your driveway every single afternoon. We protect your lawn, landscaping, and pets' safety.
