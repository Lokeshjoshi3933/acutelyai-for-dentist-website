When to Call for Trimming?

Regular tree trimming is the single most important thing you can do to keep a tree healthy, safe, and beautiful. Call us when:

  • Branches are rubbing against your roof or siding.
  • Low-hanging limbs block your driveway or sidewalk.
  • The canopy is too dense, restricting sunlight to your lawn.
  • You see dead, hanging, or diseased branches.

How We Handle It

Anyone can hack at a branch with a saw, but improper cuts can permanently damage a tree or invite pests. Our trained arborist crew knows exactly where to make the cut at the branch collar so the tree heals properly.

We use ropes to lower large limbs safely, and we never use climbing spikes on trees we are pruning, as spikes introduce disease. After trimming, we chip all the brush and clean your yard completely.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can you trim oak trees year-round?

To prevent Oak Wilt, we strictly follow city and arborist guidelines, which often means avoiding trimming oaks during peak transmission months unless there is an immediate hazard, and painting all cuts immediately.

Do you haul the brush?

Yes, we chip and haul all brush and debris generated from the pruning process.
