When to Call Us?

Texas storms can be ruthless. When high winds or lightning bring down massive trees, the resulting damage can trap vehicles or crush your roof.

  • Trees fallen on houses or roofs
  • Trees blocking driveways or roads
  • Large "widowmaker" branches hanging loose
  • Trees leaning severely after high winds

How We Handle Emergencies

In an emergency, safety is the number one priority. Stay clear of the downed tree, especially if there are any downed power lines nearby (call the utility company first if wires are involved).

When you call Lone Star Canopy, we immediately assess the situation and dispatch a crew with the necessary rigging and safety gear. We stabilize the hazardous tree, dismantle it safely without causing further damage, and clear your property.

Frequently Asked Questions

Will my insurance cover this?

Most homeowners insurance policies cover tree removal if the tree has fallen on a covered structure (like your house or fence). We provide detailed invoices and photos to help you file your claim.
