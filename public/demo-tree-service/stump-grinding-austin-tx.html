Why Remove That Old Stump?

After a tree is cut down, the stump remains. While leaving it might seem harmless, stumps often cause more problems than the tree itself.

  • Pest Magnet: Rotting wood attracts termites and carpenter ants that can migrate to your home.
  • Trip Hazard: Hidden roots and low stumps are dangerous for children playing in the yard.
  • Mowing Obstacle: Navigating a lawnmower around a stump is tedious and risks damaging your blades.
  • New Growth: Some species continuously sprout ugly new shoots from the stump.

How We Handle It

We use heavy-duty stump grinders to chew the wood down 4 to 6 inches below grade level. This ensures the stump is completely gone and allows you to plant grass or pour concrete over the area.

We can also "chase" large surface roots that are ruining your lawn or lifting your sidewalks.

Frequently Asked Questions

What happens to the wood chips?

Stump grinding generates a large pile of wood chips and dirt. We can use this mixture to backfill the hole, or we can haul it away for an additional fee depending on your preference.
