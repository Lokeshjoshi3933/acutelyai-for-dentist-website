When Do You Need Tree Removal?

Removing a tree is often a last resort, but it becomes necessary when a tree poses a threat to your home, family, or other healthy trees on your property.

  • The tree is dead or dying from disease or pests.
  • Major storm damage has compromised the trunk.
  • The tree is leaning dangerously toward a house or power lines.
  • Roots are causing severe damage to a foundation.

How We Handle It

Tree removal is dangerous work. Our fully insured crew uses specialized rigging equipment to take trees down piece by piece. This ensures heavy logs are lowered slowly and safely, without damaging roofs, fences, or your lawn.

Once the tree is down, we chip the brush, haul the heavy wood away, and rake the area clean. You won't be left with a mess.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need a permit in Austin?

Austin has specific tree protection ordinances (especially for Heritage Trees). We will help you determine if a permit is needed before we start.

Do you haul the wood away?

Yes, complete cleanup and wood removal are included in our standard estimate unless you request to keep the firewood.
