Concrete Repair & Replacement

Over time, soil movement, tree roots, and heavy loads can cause concrete to crack, sink, or flake. We offer honest evaluations to help you determine if your concrete can be repaired or if a full tear-out and replacement is necessary.

While minor surface scaling or hairline cracks can sometimes be patched or resurfaced, severe structural cracking and sinking almost always require the section to be broken up and re-poured to prevent further property damage or tripping hazards.

Our team uses heavy machinery to safely break out the damaged concrete, hauls it away, stabilizes the sub-base, and pours a fresh, reinforced slab that will stand the test of time.
